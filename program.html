Mat Etiquette

Preparing For Your Sadhana

Essential mindful guidelines to maximize prana flow, joint mobility, and shala harmony.

2.5-Hr Fasting Window

Allow your digestive Agni to settle so energy flows freely through the Sushumna nadi during bandha activations.

Organic Breathable Wear

Pure unbleached cotton or flexible linen is encouraged across our infrared heated cedarwood shalas.

Digital Mauna (Silence)

Deposit phones inside our teak cubbies before entering to preserve the 432Hz acoustic frequency undisturbed.

Listen To Ahimsa

Never force an alignment. Signal your Acharya for teak blocks, bolsters, or gentle modifications whenever needed.
