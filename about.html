Our Story

Founded In 2008 To Restore Sacred Stillness & Structural Vitality

Stackly Yoga Coaching Platform began as an intimate sunrise Hatha shala in Salem and blossomed into a premier multi-disciplinary sanctuary uniting classical Himalayan yoga with 3D joint biomechanics and Ayurvedic therapy.

Today, over 4,200 active seekers practice across our sunlit cork shalas, aerial silk domes, and 432Hz acoustic sound chambers — guided by E-RYT 500 Acharyas and clinical yoga therapists.
