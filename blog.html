Daily Sutras

Quick Notes From Our Acharyas

Three-minute micro-practices you can apply on your mat or at your desk today.

01 • MORNING GROUNDING

10 Sun Salutations Before Screens

Step onto your mat before checking notifications. Synchronizing Surya Namaskar with Ujjayi breath sets your circadian cortisol rhythm for the entire day.

02 • POSTURE CUE

Root Through The Four Corners Of Pada

In standing postures, press evenly through the big-toe mound, inner heel, pinky-toe mound, and outer heel to awaken the arches and protect your knees.

03 • EVENING PRANA

Viparita Karani (Legs Up The Wall)

Spend 8 minutes with your legs resting vertically against a wall before bed to drain lymphatic fatigue and signal deep parasympathetic rest.
